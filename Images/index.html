History of Animation

Animation has a rich history that dates back to the early 20th century. It has evolved from simple hand-drawn techniques to complex computer-generated imagery (CGI).

Early Beginnings

The history of animation can be traced back to the early 1900s when animators began experimenting with hand-drawn techniques. One of the earliest examples of animation is the short film "Gertie the Dinosaur," created by Winsor McCay in 1914.

How Animation Started

Before animation became commonly used in television and film, it was mostly used for short-term entertainment.

Examples of Early Animation

  • The Enchanted Drawing (Indie Short Film)
