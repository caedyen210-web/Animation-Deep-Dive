Hand-Drawn Animation

Hand-drawn animation is created by drawing each frame individually on paper or digitally.

  1. Spirited Away
  2. Lion King
  3. Akira
  4. The Iron Giant
  5. Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs

CGI

Computer-generated imagery (CGI) animation is created using computer software to create and manipulate digital images and scenes.

  1. Toy Story
  2. Puss and Boots the Last Wish
  3. Avatar
  4. Planet of the Apes
  5. Pirates of the Caribbean (Davey Jones)

Stop Motion (Claymation)

Stop motion animation is created by physically manipulating objects frame by frame to create the illusion of movement.

  1. Wallece and Gromit
  2. Fantastic Mr. Foc
  3. Nightmare Before Christmas
  4. Coraline
  5. Kubo and the Two Strings

Anime is a style of animation that originated in Japan and is characterized by colorful artwork, fantastical themes, and vibrant characters.

  1. Princess Mononoke
  2. Mob Psycho 100
  3. JoJos Bizarre Adventure
  4. Jujistu Kaisen
