Styles of Animation

Over the years, animation has developed multiple different styles, which brings new life to the industry. Some examples of these unique styles are shown below.

Best Animation Styles

Some examples of animation styles are as follows:

  • Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse
  • Arcane
  • Treasure Planet
  • Cowboy Bebop

Hand-Drawn Animation

Hand-drawn animation is created by drawing each frame individually on paper or digitally.

Top Five Hand-Drawn Projects

  1. Spirited Away
  2. The Lion King
  3. Akira
  4. The Iron Giant
  5. Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs

CGI

Computer-generated imagery (CGI) animation is created using computer software to create and manipulate digital images and scenes.

Top Five CGI Animations

  1. Toy Story
  2. Puss in Boots: The Last Wish
  3. Avatar
  4. Planet of the Apes
  5. Pirates of the Caribbean (Davy Jones)

Stop Motion (Claymation)

Stop motion animation is created by physically manipulating objects frame by frame to create the illusion of movement.

Top Five Stop Motion Animations

  1. Wallace and Gromit
  2. Fantastic Mr. Fox
  3. The Nightmare Before Christmas
  4. Coraline
  5. Kubo and the Two Strings

Anime

Anime is a style of animation that originated in Japan and is characterized by colorful artwork, fantastical themes, and vibrant characters.

Top Five Anime Style Animations

  1. Princess Mononoke
  2. Mob Psycho 100
  3. JoJo's Bizarre Adventure
  4. Jujutsu Kaisen
  5. Demon Slayer
